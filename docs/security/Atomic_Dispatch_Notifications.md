# Atomic dispatch notifications

Implemented bounded behavior from published baseline 42f448e45f822ad37f9e0980f860998d7f8ddd4b. Assignment, bulk driver status and single-driver status now persist eligible notifications in their existing Order/Tracking/analytics transaction. The exact Tracking/order source and owner-company recipient membership are reloaded through the transaction. Active matching tenant/company/membership, tenant bridge, branch and drivers.telemetry permission are required; ambiguous or ineligible recipients are suppressed without choosing a membership or broadening company access. Existing initiating actor permissions, scopes, transition rules and cash restrictions remain.

Tracking/order compound identity and notification source/recipient uniqueness prevent duplicate accepted source rows. New/changed populated notification references are constrained; the source FK and completeness CHECK are NOT VALID and do not certify history. Accepted source, recipient and content cannot be retargeted; legacy notifications cannot be adopted. Existing read marking and configured retention deletion remain allowed. Uniqueness applies to retained notification rows, not an invented indefinite delivery ledger. Normal covered operations never replay old Tracking creation after retention: stale preconditions reject, equal assignment is a no-op, and delivery only reloads rows.

Actual notification persistence failure rolls back the complete covered batch, including previously written rows, Tracking and analytics. The existing bulk operation is all-or-nothing within that request; independent requests remain independently committed. Ineligible-recipient suppression is not a database failure and does not cancel an otherwise authorized transition. No manual transition policy is restored.

After commit the wrappers pass only internal committed notification IDs. Realtime reloads the notification, Tracking/order and current assigned recipient context and verifies live session/permission before delivery. It creates no notification and accepts no event content or ownership payload. driver:notification and driver:notifications:unread-count retain their payload contracts. Single-driver status now uses the same notification behavior as bulk status. A crash before emission leaves a durable own-context list entry; socket delivery is best-effort and may be missed or duplicated, not exactly-once. Retention-deleted, missing, legacy unaccepted or inconsistent sources suppress delivery. Final-read revocation races remain; an accepted source does not depend on the initiating user's continued login.

Migration 20261003150000_dispatch_notification_identity is additive, without backfill or historical ownership inference. Apply migration before new writers; stop old dispatch writers/emitters before enabling this flow because old emission still separately creates rows. Rollback must preserve this authorization/atomic persistence or contain affected dispatch mutations; it must not restore user-global delivery. No migration was applied outside disposable test storage.
