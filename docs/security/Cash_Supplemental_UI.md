Proposers inspect their own proposals; checkers inspect the selected company's proposals within their ceiling. Candidate pages are bounded before eligibility filtering, so a page may be empty with a next cursor. Cursors bind tenant/company/entity/actor membership/kind/view; changing authority requires fresh server validation.
