# Journal operational dimension ownership

Current enforced behavior in covered backend paths: manual draft creation and retries require fresh selected finance authority and each referenced operational action/scope (Manual_Draft_Idempotency.md). The transaction locks and reloads authoritative references before receipt access or business writes. Finance permission alone grants no operational record access. Ordinary balanced reference-free drafts remain supported; posting, reversal, automatic rule acceptance, accounting mappings and pricing/FX remain contained.

Migration `20261002200000_journal_operational_dimensions` adds nullable journal-line tenant/company bridges, a tenant/company/journal index, two unique paired-reference targets and eight RESTRICT compound foreign keys. The entity bridge must equal the line's existing legal entity; referenced orders must have that tenant and finance owner company; customers/warehouses must have that tenant; providers must belong to that company; legs must belong to the exact referenced order. When present together, order/customer and leg/carrier must equal their stored relationships. Warehouse company ownership is not invented. These constraints prove equality and existence, not active status, object permission, legal classification, approved service charges, carrier acceptance or accounting treatment.

Two NOT VALID checks protect new/changed lines: a populated operational dimension requires legalEntityId, tenantId and companyId; partial tenant/company bridges are forbidden; a leg requires an order. Branch/cost/profit-center tags remain forbidden until owned financial configuration and acceptance are designed. Null optional dimensions intentionally skip their own MATCH SIMPLE foreign key. All-null tenant/company bridges remain permitted only on reference-free lines, whose journal/account entity consistency is independently protected. Existing rows are not mapped or certified. New manual lines derive bridges from selected authoritative context; contained reversal and automatic writer code derives bridges from its already validated owner/entity, without impersonating a human or receiving a bypass. Those unavailable execution paths are not claimed restored or independently tested as accepted accounting workflows.

Final list/detail/cursor predicates and duplicate-result graph validation reject incomplete, unowned or conflicting dimensions and unaccepted branch/center tags. A whole journal is hidden rather than returning partial lines or totals. Reference-free legacy lines can remain visible only under the existing active selected entity, owned document/accounts and reversal restrictions. Projections still omit raw dimension IDs, ownership bridges, receipt data and metadata; event/response shapes and exact decimal outputs are unchanged. An active configured carrier is required for populated carrier dimensions; pausing/disabling it can hide affected journals until a separately reviewed historical visibility policy exists. Reads do not repair or mutate records.

Application resource checks and share locks are distinct from database ownership constraints. Locks prevent retargeting through the validating transaction; foreign keys protect populated references after commit. Membership/permission/status changes can still race a fresh check; no immediate distributed revocation claim is made. Historical NOT VALID rows, other business source relationships, general posted-line immutability and malicious privileged SQL remain unresolved. This does not establish complete isolation, approval, provider guarantees or exactly-once external delivery.

Rollout requires this new additive migration and matching Prisma artifacts/source. No committed migration is edited and no existing database execution/backfill is authorized. Older writers with populated dimensions but no bridges now reject safely; legacy reversal/source records with unaccepted tags cannot be restored merely for compatibility. Rollback must retain equivalent ownership/policy enforcement or disable the affected paths, never restore unscoped writes. Constraint validation/historical certification requires a separately authorized database plan; do not alter posted history to make validation pass.

Offline Prisma WASM syntax, selected manual source/SQL comparison and PostgreSQL catalog checks are separate evidence; the text checklist is not full semantic schema-to-SQL equivalence. Only synthetic data in newly owned disposable PostgreSQL is used. No real provider, Redis, storage, deployed infrastructure or client/device validation is claimed. Clients remain deferred.
