## Consolidated decisions — not engineering defaults

- Pricing/FX: approved rate provenance/effective time, precision/rounding, immutable price/source acceptance and treatment of stale/changed prices; shared region/zone/SLA classification and mutation authority.
- Financial approval: company-scoped independent checker eligibility for configuration, rules, journal/reversal/refund/payment-run/settlement proposals; approved accounting mappings and source-document/provider matching; activation/supersession/cancellation/revocation of accepted work. Separate humans is already required, not a new threshold decision.
- Recovery: permitted reconciliation evidence, cancellation/reacceptance and uncertain provider/storage outcomes; never equate timeout with failure or auto-replay using a fresh ID.
- Privileged/operational authority: grant/delegation ceilings, deliberate tenant/company/warehouse provisioning and assignment eligibility, manual transition/override and recipient policy. Existing owner-company notifications are unchanged; no cross-company policy inferred.
- Lifecycle: production retention/legal hold/erasure and platform notifications; refresh compromise versus benign ambiguous retry treatment. None grants user-global access. Existing operational retention remains.
